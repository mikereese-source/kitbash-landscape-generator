# LG-090 Glacial-style valley walls on Mountains
Type: feature | Milestone: Model realism | Phase: B2 Model realism | Size: L | Priority: P2
Status: done | Lane: side | Needs Blender: yes | Mike review: yes
Depends on: LG-079, LG-080 | Blocks: LG-082

## Why
Mike, 2026-11-25, testing LG-080 in 5.3: Glacial gets really nice cliffs, and Mountains would get better shapes from the same approach. Glacial draws its walls on purpose (nodes.py recipe 9): a valley line, a wall that stands straight up from the floor and rounds over at an even rim, and its own hard map so erosion keeps the wall sheer. Mountains has no valley line, so its cliffs only appear where noise ridges happen to get steep: broken and scattered, not the tall even headwalls and trough sides of real glaciated ranges.

## Done when
- Mountains gets drawn walls along its main valleys: cirque headwalls at valley heads and trough sides lower down, built from the Glacial wall profile: the same formula, checked by a test (Mike, 2026-11-26: a node tree and numpy can't share code)
- The new walls shed as hard rock in the rockfall, so talus (LG-080) collects at their feet (Mike, 2026-11-26: carved after the erosion passes, so the hard map would change nothing)
- Glacial looks the same (T48 numbers unchanged)
- Mountains is no worse on the accepted measures: teeth/horns not above main, over 60 deg not above main (real 11.7%), upper-third crest at or above T49's floor; relief (1-99%) may rise up to the board's +9% (Mike, 2026-11-26: real ranges have 2-3x our relief; Height stays exact)
- A new measure for tall continuous walls rises toward the reference: the share of over-60-deg ground in unbroken faces 50 m or taller (tall50; Mike, 2026-11-26); its test sits at the measured board value
- Wall depth scales with Erosion Amount; a Mountains Wall Depth slider (0 = no walls, default = the c205 walls at Amount 0.8, max = twice that) sets it; T51 checks that depth follows both (Mike, 2026-11-26)
- Board beside a glaciated-range reference (cirque headwall + trough wall), Preview vs High alike, suite passes, code review
